# Modes
The d-pad picks what the crank does.
- UP: Helm. Crank turns the wheel. Press UP again to recentre the camera.
- RIGHT: Trim. Crank sheets the sail in and out. Press again for the next sail.
- LEFT: Hoist. Crank raises and lowers the sail. Press again for the next sail.
- DOWN: Look. Crank swings the camera all the way round the boat. Tap again to switch to raising and lowering it. Hold to bring it back to centre.

# Autopilot (A)
Hold A for about half a second to switch the autopilot on or off. A compass shows in the bar with the heading it holds.
- Tap A to step the heading round, skipping any heading into the wind.
- Turn the crank to nudge the heading.

# Auto trim (B)
Hold B for about half a second to switch auto trim on or off. A gear shows in the bar while it is on.
- Tap B once to drop or lift the anchor.
- Mash B to paddle: a short push up to a slow speed, then a rest.

# After a capsize or wreck
Press A to start again.

# Racing
Sail the course marks. The clock stops at the finish and shows your time. Press A to come back to this menu.

# Journey
Buoys keep appearing ahead, one after another. Round each one in the direction shown to count it; buoys you have passed drop away behind you. The pause menu shows how many you have passed.

# System menu
Use the Playdate menu button for boat, weather, restart and main menu.
